Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 July 2026

Wasiati ("we", "us") is a service of Wasiati Inc. that helps you prepare a Sharia-compliant will and have it witnessed, sealed and — when the time comes — released to the people you name. A will is among the most personal documents a person ever writes. This policy explains what we collect, why, and what we will and will not do with it. Questions and requests: privacy@wasiati.com.

1. What we collect

  • Your account. Email address, mobile number, region (Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Canada or the United States) and a password we store only as a cryptographic hash.
  • Your will. The heirs, shares and bequests you record; assets you choose to list; funeral and burial wishes; guardianship of minor children; personal written messages; and any video messages you record or upload.
  • People you name. The names, phone numbers and email addresses of the witnesses, trustee and heir contacts you add. You are responsible for having their permission before you give us their details; we use them only to run the will's own processes (invitations, one-time codes, release confirmations).
  • Identity verification. Verifying your identity before a will is sealed is handled by a specialist identity-verification provider; we store the outcome of the check, not your identity documents.
  • Death claims. When someone reports a death, we collect the claimant's name and contact details and the death certificate they upload, so a human reviewer can assess the claim.
  • Evidence of signing. When you sign your will, and when a witness signs or a trustee confirms, we record the time, IP address and browser details. These form part of the will's evidentiary record.
  • Technical data. Ordinary server logs and the information needed to keep the service secure and rate-limited.

2. What we use it for

To provide the service itself: computing fara'id shares, delivering one-time codes by SMS and email, sealing and storing your will, reviewing death claims by hand, and releasing the will only through the process you set. We also use data to bill you, to prevent fraud and abuse, and to meet legal obligations. We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use the contents of your will for advertising of any kind.

3. The vault

Vault items are end-to-end encrypted on your device under a passphrase that never reaches us. We cannot read them, and if the passphrase is lost, we cannot recover them — not even for you. Vault items are not released through the heir portal.

4. Videos and documents

Video messages and uploaded documents are encrypted at rest and scanned before they can be downloaded. Video messages are released only through the will's release process, after a claim has been reviewed and approved by a person.

5. Payments

Card payments are processed by Stripe. Your card number goes to Stripe, not to us — Wasiati never stores it. We keep records of your plan, invoices and payment status.

6. When we share

  • Service providers who process data on our instructions: cloud hosting and storage, SMS and email delivery, payment processing, and identity verification.
  • People named on your will — but only what the release process entitles them to, only after a human reviewer approves a claim, and only through the safeguards the product enforces (safety window, heir confirmations or trustee override).
  • Authorities, where the law genuinely requires it.

7. Retention

We keep your data while your account is active. An executed will and the evidentiary record around it are kept for as long as they may be needed to serve their purpose. You may request deletion of your account at privacy@wasiati.com; we will delete what we can and tell you plainly what we must keep, and why.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live — including under Saudi Arabia's Personal Data Protection Law, Qatar's Data Protection Law, Canada's PIPEDA and applicable US state privacy laws — you may have rights to access, correct, export and delete your personal information, and to complain to a supervisory authority. Write to us and we will honour these rights without making you fight for them.

9. Security

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Sensitive actions require one-time codes sent to the phone on record. Access inside Wasiati is scoped: the release of a will is gated by human review and by the confirmations of the people you named, not by a switch someone can quietly flip.

10. Children

Wasiati is not directed at children and we do not knowingly open accounts for minors. Information about minor children (such as guardianship wishes) is provided by the account holder as part of their will.

11. Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we will tell you before it takes effect. The date at the top is always the date of the current version.
