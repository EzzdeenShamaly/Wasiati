2. The service

Wasiati helps you compose a will, computes inheritance shares according to the fara'id rules of the school you select, lets the witnesses and trustee you name confirm by one-time SMS codes, and seals the finished will. When a death is reported, a claim is reviewed by a person; if it is approved, the will is released to the people named on it through the confirmations and safety checks built into the product. Nothing is released automatically.

You are responsible for ensuring that your will satisfies the formalities that apply to you. We recommend having a qualified professional in your jurisdiction review any document this service produces, particularly if your estate or family situation is complex.

4. Your responsibilities
